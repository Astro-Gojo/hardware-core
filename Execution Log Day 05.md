Focus: The Step-by-Step Flow of Hardware/Software Interaction

1. The Inbound Flow (Sensor → Software)
Physical → Electrical → ADC → Register → Variable.

The ADC acts as the bridge between continuous analog voltages and discrete digital numbers.

2. The Outbound Flow (Software → Action)
Decision → Register → Pin Voltage → Physical Actuator.

Software control is fundamentally the act of updating memory-mapped registers to trigger electrical changes.

3. Component Roles
ADC: The "Translator" of incoming signals.

Registers: The "Shared Interface" or "Mailboxes" where software and hardware exchange information.
